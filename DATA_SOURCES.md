# Data provenance

The prototype uses 2024 ATP and WTA match records from the archival mirror at `Aneeshers/tennis-sackmann-archive`, derived from Jeff Sackmann / Tennis Abstract datasets.

- Source: https://github.com/Aneeshers/tennis-sackmann-archive
- Upstreams: `JeffSackmann/tennis_atp` and `JeffSackmann/tennis_wta`
- License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0
- Snapshot: 2024 tour-level match CSVs, downloaded 2026-08-15
- Transformation: `scripts/build_data.py`
- Venue coordinates: manually curated public venue locations, approximate map coordinates
