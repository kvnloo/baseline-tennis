## Baseline mobile product direction

### Design stance
A mobile-first tennis intelligence tool that combines editorial sports graphics with traceable analytical workflows.

### Screens
- Explore: map-led tournament discovery with filters and compact summary metrics.
- Charts: player-head scatter plots inspired by the supplied TennisViz reference, redesigned for touch and small screens.
- Ask: grounded statistical reports with comparisons, methodology, filters, and follow-up queries.

### Key choices
- Layout: strict single-column phone flow with four-item bottom navigation.
- Typography: bold condensed-feeling sans headlines with small functional labels.
- Color: warm monochrome surfaces with one tennis-ball lime accent.
- Interaction: venue selection, chart-mode chips, report follow-ups, and persistent query composer.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: mobile scanability, clear information hierarchy, and traceable statistical answers.
- Weak at: dense scatter plots still require progressive zoom or selective labeling for larger player sets.
